## Gumroad / Payhip

**Name:** Paycheck Planner: Budget by Paycheck (Excel + Google Sheets)

**Summary (one line):** The spreadsheet from the Paycheck Math videos: give every bill a paycheck, plan for surprise costs, and see your debt-free date.

**Description:**
```
Most budgets assume you get paid once a month. You don't. The Paycheck Planner is built around when your money actually arrives.

WHAT'S INSIDE (5 tabs)
• Paycheck Plan: list your bills and due dates. Each bill is assigned to paycheck 1 or 2, then the two paychecks are evened out so there's no "broke week." It shows exactly what's left for your goal.
• Surprise Fund: list yearly costs (gifts, car repairs, renewals). It tells you what to set aside each paycheck.
• Debt Payoff: up to 6 debts. Pick snowball or avalanche and get your debt-free date, total interest, and how much you save versus paying minimums.
• Paydays: enter your first payday of the year to find your 3-paycheck months, and how much extra money they bring.
• Start Here: a 5-step guide and your key numbers on one page.

HOW IT WORKS
Yellow cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates. Example numbers from the videos are filled in, so you can see it working before you type your own.

WORKS WITH
Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets (File → Import in Google Sheets). No macros, no add-ons.

Instant download. For personal use.

Educational tool, not financial advice. Results are estimates; your lender's actual interest and minimums may differ.
```
