# Paycheck Math: channel plan (Dec 2026 – Feb 2027)

**Niche:** Personal finance for regular paycheck earners: budgeting, debt payoff and saving, always with real worked numbers.
**Format:** Faceless animated explainers (kinetic type and data visuals, AI voice). Long-form plus Shorts cut from them.
**Money without ads:** a digital product the videos naturally lead to, then affiliates and sponsors.

## Why this niche
| Factor | Why it fits your goal |
|---|---|
| Timing | Searches for "budget", "pay off debt" and "money reset" peak in January, and tax-refund questions follow in February. Videos published in December are indexed before that peak. |
| Works with no ads | Every video ends with "plug your numbers into the planner". The tool sells itself because viewers just watched it work. |
| Advertiser value | Finance is among the best-paying niches for ads (a few times what gaming or vlogs earn) and for sponsors, so the channel is worth more once you qualify for ads later. |
| Faceless-friendly | Numbers on screen *are* the content. No face, studio or B-roll needed. |
| Your edge | You're a motion designer. Clean, honest animated numbers stand out in a niche full of talking heads and stock footage. |

**Name check:** before creating the channel, search YouTube, Instagram and TikTok for "Paycheck Math". If it's taken, the engine's brand is one line (`BRAND` in `engine/player.html`). Alternatives: *Paycheck Plan*, *Money by the Numbers*, *Two Paychecks*.

## Revenue: the no-ads stack, in order
1. **Paycheck Planner** (`products/Paycheck Planner.xlsx`, already built and tested)
   - Sell it on **Gumroad** or **Payhip**, both free to start with a fee per sale. Suggested price: $9, with a $5 launch price through January.
   - Also list it on **Etsy**. Budget templates are a big search category there, so it can sell even to people who never see the channel.
   - Make a Google Sheets copy by uploading the .xlsx to Google Drive, then *File → Save as Google Sheets*. Offer both formats in the listing.
   - Listing images: use the video's paycheck-card and payoff frames (`ep01/build/still-*.png`).
2. **Affiliate links** (apply in week 1, since approval takes days): budgeting apps, high-yield savings accounts, credit-score tools. Many finance brands run programs through networks such as Impact, CJ or FlexOffers. Check each program's current terms, and always disclose: *"Some links are affiliate links; I may earn a commission at no cost to you."*
3. **YouTube fan funding** at 500 subscribers (Super Thanks, memberships). The bar stays the same after Feb 1.
4. **Sponsorships** around 1–5k subscribers. Fintech apps sponsor small, focused finance channels. Use a business email in your About tab.

**Ad revenue:** to get the old bar (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours), you must **apply by Jan 31, 2027**. From Feb 1 it doubles to 8,000 hours. From a cold start that's a stretch, so the plan doesn't depend on it. If you're close in late January, push. Otherwise keep building toward 8,000.

**Honest expectations:** a new channel can't count on revenue by February. Realistically, early income is a handful to a few dozen planner sales (Etsy can add more) plus small affiliate earnings. What makes it work is consistency through the January search peak.

## Production cadence (built into the engine)
Each episode takes about 1–2 hours of your time: write `script.json`, render, review, upload.
- **2 long videos a week** (3–8 min) and **3–4 Shorts a week** (cut from the long ones; ep01-short is the template).
- Upload at the same times each week. Tue/Fri is a good default for finance. Check *Analytics → Audience → When your viewers are on YouTube* after a month.

## Rules that protect the channel
- **Original every time.** YouTube demonetizes mass-produced, repetitive "inauthentic" content. Every episode needs its own script, numbers and visuals. Don't publish template copies.
- **Education, not advice.** Keep the on-screen disclaimer and avoid "you should buy X". Worked examples and math are safe ground.
- **Check every number.** The engine makes numbers look authoritative, so they have to be right (ep01's and ep02's were verified in code).
- **The voice** is Kokoro `af_heart`, an open model under the Apache-2.0 license, so commercial use is fine. Keep the same voice for brand consistency. Your own voice is an option later; *Say It Slower* proves you can do it.

## Weekly metrics to watch (YouTube Studio)
- **Click-through rate**: under 4% means the thumbnail or title needs work. Test with *Test & Compare* (thumbnails).
- **Average view duration / % viewed**: find the drop-off point and fix that kind of moment in the next script.
- **Planner clicks → sales**: use a separate link per video (e.g. Gumroad `?ref=ep01`) to see which topics sell.
