# Upload calendar

Every episode and Short with a date. Upload in YouTube Studio, choose **Schedule**, and set the date below. Pick **one posting time** and keep it. If unsure, 4 PM your local time is a reasonable default; check *Analytics → Audience* after a month and adjust.

- Episodes go out in their original order, so each "next time" teaser points to the next video.
- Shorts go out 2 days after their episode (1 day for Episode 1), with **Related video** set to that episode.
- Dec 18 and Dec 22 are left open (holiday week). Episode 7 (holiday debt) lands right after Christmas, Episode 8 (2027 reset) on Dec 29 for New Year's searches, and Episode 19 (Valentine's) before Feb 14.
- Titles and descriptions are in `UPLOAD.md`. `upload-calendar.ics` imports these as all-day reminders into Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.
