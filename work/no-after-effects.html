04 · Character motion

No After Effects

Bez pops out of a keyboard keycap, dives into the monitor and builds a motion graphic in a fictional animation app by hopping on its buttons and keyframes, landing on every beat. The film itself is made entirely in code, with no After Effects.

Format12s · 1920×1080
RoleCharacter, UI design, animation, sound
ProductFictional app “Loopwork”
TypeSelf-initiated

The challenge

UI promos usually show the interface on its own. This one needed a character so the product feels alive, and the motion had to follow the music.

The approach

  • Every hop changes something: it presses a tool, stamps a keyframe or plays back the timeline, and 17 landings sit on a 120 BPM grid.
  • The app logic is real: stamped keys snap the comp to a pose, and the playhead rewinds to play back the eased move.
  • Squash on landing and stretch in the air give a small character real weight.

Craft notes

  • A deterministic, frame-accurate pipeline: HTML/SVG animation rendered frame by frame, then encoded with sound.
  • Original beat and sound effects, with the timing driven by the same timeline as the animation.
