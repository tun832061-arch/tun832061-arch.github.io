Clown Club — Pack & Unpack

Seven tees leave the rail, fold into a box, get taped and shipped, then unbox top-down and land back on the rail. The whole order journey in 15 seconds.

Format15s · 1920×1080
RoleConcept, design, animation, sound
BrandClown Club (my label)
CaptionsPick it. Pack it. Ship it. Wear it.

The challenge

Drawn clothes looked cheap, so the garments had to come from real photos while staying fully animated: they fly, fold and stack on the beat.

The approach

  • One photographed tee, cut out and recoloured into seven colourways that keep the real fabric shading.
  • The story is a single camera move: rail → box → push-in → cut to top-down unboxing → back to the rail.
  • Each tee drops into the box on an eighth note; tape, label and box bounces sit on the beat.

Craft notes

  • Squash and stretch on the box, with motion blur on every fast move.
  • Four story captions keep the film readable with the sound off.
