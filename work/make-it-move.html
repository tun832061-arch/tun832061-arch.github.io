What changed

  • Found the cause of v1's stray blocks — a chrome text fill breaking on individually animated letters — and fixed it per glyph.
  • Re-composed for vertical: larger type, frame furniture, a progress hairline.
  • The sphere is now a motif: it drops in, zooms through to the next beat and lands as the period of "MOVE."
  • An original score with hits on every cut, mastered for social.
