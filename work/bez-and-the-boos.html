Bez & the Boos

Bez the mascot wanders onto a dark stage and wakes up a band, one beat at a time: a ghost on organ, a pumpkin on drums, a bat on sax and a cat on double bass.

Format15s · 1920×1080
RoleCharacters, animation, music
OccasionHalloween
TypeSelf-initiated

The challenge

Seasonal posts get one scroll's worth of attention. The film had to be instantly readable, a little funny and built to loop.

The approach

  • Each new musician enters on its own instrument's first note, so you hear the band build as you see it.
  • The title letters drop in on the beat, then the whole band plays together for the payoff.
  • A limited palette (charcoal, cream, one orange) keeps five characters calm on screen.

Craft notes

  • Characters and instruments are drawn in code, with every hop and hit driven from one shared beat timeline.
  • Original score with each part arranged per character.
