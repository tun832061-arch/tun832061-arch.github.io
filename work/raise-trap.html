The Raise Trap

A warm, character-led explainer that makes one money idea stick: a raise only makes you richer if your spending doesn't rise with it.

Format60s · 1920×1080
RoleScript, design, animation, sound
AudienceYoung professionals
TypeSelf-initiated

The challenge

Lifestyle inflation is invisible: no single upgrade feels like the problem. The video had to make that slow creep visible in a minute, without lecturing.

The approach

  • One character, Theo, carries the story: raise → upgrades → the chart that gives it away → the fix.
  • The key frame is a chart where income steps up and spending follows — the "what he keeps" gap never grows.
  • The fix is a single concrete action (split every raise on payday), then proved with real numbers.

Craft notes

  • Every reveal is timed to the spoken word it illustrates; captions are burned in for sound-off viewing.
  • Figures are calculated, not guessed: $300/month at 5%/yr compounded monthly → $20,402 after five years, stated on screen.
  • Original score and sound effects; warm sticker-style design system (cream, charcoal outlines, coral accents).
Process

Storyboard — approved before any animation

Figures are illustrative and not financial advice.
