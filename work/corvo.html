CORVO Knockout

A loud, punch-timed ad pack for a boxing-glove launch: one design system, three deliverables, built to stop the scroll with the sound off.

Deliverables15s 9:16 · 6s 16:9 · 15s 1:1
RoleConcept, design, animation, music
AudienceBeginner–intermediate boxers
TypeSpec ad · fictional brand

The challenge

Product ads live or die in the first second, are mostly watched muted, and have to ship in several formats at once.

The approach

  • Hook → claim → proof → detail → offer → CTA, in 15 seconds.
  • Every cut and callout lands on a beat of an original 120 BPM score; the text carries the message without sound.
  • The bumper and the square cut are built from the same design system and motion, re-laid out per format — change the price once, every cut updates.

Craft notes

  • The glove and the crow-wing mark are drawn in code — no stock photos, no licensing risk.
  • Text kept clear of the Reels/TikTok interface zones.
Process

Storyboard — six beats, approved before animation

Spec work: CORVO is a fictional brand created for this portfolio. Product, price and claims are invented.
