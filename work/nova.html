01 · Product launch

NOVA

A premium vertical launch spot for wireless earbuds, built around one shape that never cuts: a line of light becomes the case, the earbud, an equaliser, and finally the O in the NOVA logo.

Format15s · 1080×1920
RoleConcept, design, animation, sound
ChannelsReels · TikTok · Shorts
TypeSpec ad · fictional brand

The challenge

Tech launch ads tend to look the same: product spins, glow and a tagline. NOVA needed to feel premium and hold attention for 15 seconds with no footage and no 3D model.

The approach

  • One continuous shape carries the whole film, so the eye never has to look for the next thing.
  • Every move uses physical spring motion with overshoot and settle, not stock ease curves.
  • The type is short and timed to the sound hits: four lines, each with room to land.

Craft notes

  • Real motion blur: each frame blends up to 16 sub-frames across a 180° shutter, so fast moves smear like film.
  • Original synthesized score and sound design, mastered for phone speakers.
  • Text kept clear of the Reels and TikTok interface zones.

Spec work: NOVA is a fictional brand created for this portfolio.
