Creative process

How a project runs

Six steps, the same on every project. You see and approve each one before the next begins.

  1. Brief

    We agree what it has to say, who it is for, where it runs and when it is due.

  2. Concept

    A direction in words and references, so we agree on the feel before anything is designed.

  3. Storyboard

    Styleframes and an animatic timed to sound. The pacing gets fixed here, while changes are cheap.

  4. Animation

    Every shot blocked, then refined, with motion that suits the brand rather than a preset.

  5. Sound design

    Music, effects and the mix, so every cut lands and the film works on a phone speaker.

  6. Final delivery

    Masters in every format and size you need, named and ready to post.

About

Design first, then make it move.

Clown is the studio name of an independent motion designer working across brand, product, social and story.

I design, animate and make the sound for every piece myself. Much of the work is built frame by frame, often in code, which keeps the timing exact and leaves room for things a template can’t do.

Every project starts with a written brief and still frames that already look finished, so you can see where it is going before anything moves. The test for the final film is simple: it should read with the sound off and feel better with it on.
